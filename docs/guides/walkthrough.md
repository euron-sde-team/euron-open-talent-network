# Walkthrough: a tour of the demo network

A guided tour of Euron Open Talent on the local stack, using the demo data. It follows
the flows that make this a talent network rather than a feed: a profile is published on
purpose, a recruiter approaches a candidate, the candidate shares exactly the contact
channels they choose and sees every reveal, an application moves through a live pipeline,
an operator approves a recruiter, and a moderator works a case. Each step names the API
call and the code that decides it; paths are under [`apps/api/src`](../../apps/api/src/app.ts)
unless stated otherwise.

The web app is still being built. This page describes it at the level of routes; where a
page does not exist yet in [`apps/web/src/app`](../../apps/web/src/app/layout.tsx), the
API call still works and the step says so.

## 0. Start the stack and seed it

```bash
make up      # the local stack on emulated AWS (see quickstart-local.md)
make demo    # seeds the demo network and prints the logins and the shared password
```

The web app is at <http://localhost:3520> and Mailpit (where every email lands) at
<http://localhost:8036>. The seed ([`demo/seed.ts`](../../apps/api/src/demo/seed.ts)) creates
eight fictional companies on `.example` domains (six VERIFIED; Atlas Mobility and Pinecone
Games UNVERIFIED), about forty people with full profiles, open jobs with two screening
questions each, applications at every pipeline stage, approaches (pending, accepted with a
shared email, declined), conversations, completed engagements with revealed ratings, two
open complaints and two recruiter applications waiting for review. Everything goes through
the real services, so counters, notifications and moderation records are what real use
produces.

Every demo account shares one password, printed by `make demo` (it is
`TALENT_DEMO_PASSWORD` in `deploy/compose/.env`, generated by `make env`). The logins used
below:

## 1. A tour of the candidate side

Sign in as Juniper.

| Route | What it is | API behind it |

| `/jobs`, `/jobs/saved`, `/jobs/applications` | Job search, saved jobs, your applications with their timelines | `/api/jobs`, `/api/applications/mine` |
| `/companies`, `/company/[slug]` | Company pages with their jobs, people and posts | `/api/companies/*` |
| `/talent`, `/search` | The people directory with filters, and search across people, jobs and companies | `/api/search/people`, `/api/search` |

| `/notifications` | Everything that happened to you | `/api/notifications` |
| `/dashboard` | Profile views, approaches received, who has seen your contact details, job invitations, engagements and complaints | `/api/profiles/me/analytics`, `/api/approaches/received`, `/api/profiles/me/contact-log`, `/api/trust/*` |
| `/settings` | Account, message request policy, two-factor, sessions, export, delete | `/api/users/me`, `/api/auth/*` |

Two privacy details to notice on `/in/juniper-vale`:

- Juniper's open-to-work mode is RECRUITERS. Signed in as another candidate you do not see
  the signal; signed in as Harper you do (`showsOpenToWork` in
  [`domain/profile.ts`](../../apps/api/src/domain/profile.ts)).
- Her email and phone are on her profile, but no `Profile` response contains them. They
  exist only in her own `MyProfile` and, later, in a logged reveal.

## 2. Publish a profile on purpose

Nothing ever publishes a profile on someone's behalf
([ADR 0019](../architecture/adr/0019-explicit-publish.md)). To see the gate, sign up a new
account at `/signup`, confirm it from the email in Mailpit, and open `/profile/setup`.

1. Add a headline and save. Each save rebuilds the completeness score from the rows
   (`refreshDerived` in [`repositories/profile.repository.ts`](../../apps/api/src/repositories/profile.repository.ts)),
   weighted toward what a recruiter reads: headline 15, about 15, photo 10, skills 15,
   experience 20, projects 10, education 5, links 5, contact 5.
2. Try to publish (`POST /api/profiles/me/status { status: "PUBLISHED" }`). It answers
   `422 PUBLISH_INCOMPLETE` with `{ completeness, threshold }` (the threshold is
   `settings.profile.publishThreshold`, 55 by default).
3. Fill in the about text, a role (months are `YYYY-MM`) and skills, and publish again.
   Without a private contact channel it answers `422 PUBLISH_NO_CONTACT`: recruiters need a
   way to reach a published profile once the candidate says yes. Add an email under
   contact (`PUT /api/profiles/me/contact`) and publish.
4. Optionally set per-section audiences (`PUT /api/profiles/me/visibility`): experience
   for CONNECTIONS, links for RECRUITERS. A viewer outside an audience gets that section
   empty and listed in `hiddenSections`.

`UNLISTED` is the other choice: the page opens by direct link but stays out of the
directory and search. Changing the username keeps the old handle working: `/in/<old>`
answers `301 PROFILE_MOVED` and the web app redirects.

With `AI_PROVIDER=stub` (the local default) the import wizard works without a key: paste
a few sentences about your work into it and watch the status move through EXTRACTING,
GENERATING and SCORING to READY (`import:status` events), then apply the sections you
want. Badges survive only with a verbatim quote from your input
([ai.md](../architecture/ai.md)).

## 3. A recruiter approaches, the candidate consents

The full model is in
[privacy-and-contact-consent.md](../architecture/privacy-and-contact-consent.md).

**Harper approaches Juniper.** Signed in as Harper, open `/in/juniper-vale` (or find her
through `/recruiter/talent` with the open-to-work filter, which matches RECRUITERS-mode
profiles only for an ACTIVE recruiter). Send an approach: a role title, a message of at
least 30 characters, optionally a job and a salary range
(`POST /api/approaches`). In [`services/approach.service.ts`](../../apps/api/src/services/approach.service.ts)
the send:

- locks Harper's recruiter row so the quota and the pending check are exact;
- asks `approachBlock` ([`domain/trust.ts`](../../apps/api/src/domain/trust.ts)): no
  pending approach between them, no decline within the cooldown, under the daily quota,
  a published or unlisted profile, no block (a block reads as `APPROACH_NOT_PUBLISHED`, so
  the recruiter learns nothing);
- inserts the approach with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` on the one-pending-per-pair index,
  queues a moderation scan, and after commit notifies Juniper and emits `approach:new` to
  her `user:` room.

**Juniper accepts with chosen channels.** In Juniper's window the approach appears under
`/dashboard` (approaches) and in `/notifications`. Accept it sharing EMAIL and PHONE
(`POST /api/approaches/:id/accept { channels }`). Each channel must be one she has filled
in: asking for WHATSAPP, which the seed did not give her, is `400 CHANNEL_NOT_SET`. In one
transaction the approach becomes ACCEPTED (conditionally, `WHERE status = 'PENDING'`, so a
racing withdraw cannot also win), a `contact_unlocks` row records exactly those channels,
and an APPROACH thread opens with both sides ACTIVE. The conversation appears in both
people's `/messaging`.

**Harper reveals, and it is logged.** In `/recruiter/approaches`, Harper reveals the
details (`GET /api/approaches/:id/contact`). The answer carries only the shared channels,
is `Cache-Control: no-store`, and writes one `contact_access_log` row per channel shown.

**Juniper sees who looked, and takes it back.** The contact log on `/dashboard`
(`GET /api/profiles/me/contact-log`) lists the reveal: recruiter, company, channel, time.
The seed already logged a few reveals for other candidates. Revoke the unlock
(`POST /api/approaches/:id/revoke`): the conversation stays, but Harper's next reveal is
`403 CONTACT_NOT_SHARED`. Declining instead starts a cooldown before Harper can approach
again (`settings.recruiter.declineCooldownDays`); blocking from the approach ends it for
good.

## 4. Easy Apply and the live pipeline

The rules are in [hiring-pipeline.md](../architecture/hiring-pipeline.md).

**Juniper applies.** Open `/jobs`, pick an open Northwind Labs job Juniper has not applied
to, and use Easy Apply (`POST /api/jobs/:id/apply`). Every seeded job asks a required
YES or NO question and an optional text question: leave the required one empty and the
answer is `422 ANSWERS_INVALID` with `details.problems`. Optionally attach a resume (a
`RESUME` upload, by upload id). The application stores a snapshot of her profile as it is
now, so the team reviews what she submitted even if the profile changes later, and a
skill match score.

**Harper watches the board.** Open `/recruiter/jobs`, then the job (`/recruiter/jobs/[id]`):
the pipeline board (`GET /api/jobs/:id/pipeline`). The board subscribes to the job's room
(`job:subscribe`, allowed only for an ACTIVE recruiter of the company), so Juniper's new
card arrives as a `pipeline:card` event without a reload. Open the same board in a second
Harper window to see a move by one appear in the other.

**Harper moves the card.** Move it to SCREENING, then INTERVIEW, with a team note
(`POST /api/applications/:id/move`). `canMoveStage`
([`domain/pipeline.ts`](../../apps/api/src/domain/pipeline.ts)) allows forward moves and
corrections back to an earlier open stage; REJECTED needs a reason; HIRED and WITHDRAWN are
final. The update is conditional on the stage the card was read in, so if a teammate moved
it first the answer is `409 APPLICATION_CHANGED`.

**Juniper sees the timeline.** `/jobs/applications` shows each stage change with its time
(`GET /api/applications/mine`). The API pushes `application:stage` to her `user:` room and
an `APPLICATION_STAGE` notification. Team notes never reach her: the candidate view is a
separate presenter that has no notes and no ratings.

After a hire, either side can propose an engagement from `/recruiter/engagements` or
`/dashboard`; ratings open only once both sides confirmed it and it completed, and stay
hidden until both have rated or the window closes.

## 5. An operator approves a recruiter

Sign in as Rowan (RECRUITER_OPS) and open `/admin`. Only the areas this role may open are
shown (overview, recruiter approvals, companies, jobs); asking the API for another area is
`403 FORBIDDEN_AREA`.

1. **Recruiter approvals** (`/admin?area=recruiters`) lists the seed's two waiting
   applications, oldest first: one on Pinecone Games' own domain, one using a free-mail
   address at Atlas Mobility. The rows show `domainMatches` and `freeMail` as evidence; both
   companies are UNVERIFIED, which is why neither was approved automatically.
2. Approve the first and reject the second with a note asking for a work address
   (`POST /api/admin/recruiters/:id/review`). A rejection without a note is
   `400 NOTE_REQUIRED`. Each applicant is notified and emailed (see Mailpit).
3. To see auto-approval instead, sign up a fresh account, open `/recruiter/join`, choose
   Northwind Labs and a work email on `northwind.example`, and enter the six-digit code
   from Mailpit. Northwind is VERIFIED with domain auto-approval on, so the account is
   ACTIVE at once and never reaches the queue.

The checklist an operator follows is in [recruiter-verification.md](recruiter-verification.md).

## 6. A moderator works a case

The local stack moderates with the deterministic stub (`MODERATION_PROVIDER=stub`), which
scores text by markers ([`apps/ai-stub`](../../apps/ai-stub/README.md)).

1. As any member, post something containing `[[mod:harassment=0.97]]`
   (`POST /api/posts`). The `moderation.scan` job scores it, `decideModeration`
   ([`domain/moderation-decision.ts`](../../apps/api/src/domain/moderation-decision.ts))
   compares 0.97 with the harassment thresholds (review 0.5, hide 0.85), and the post is
   hidden at once with an AI case opened. The author is told and may appeal.
2. Sign in as Quinn and open `/moderation` (or `/admin?area=moderation`). The case shows
   the snapshot, the score and the category. Quinn's open-case badge updated live
   (`moderation:queue` to the `staff` room).
3. Decide: RESTORE if it was a false positive, REMOVE otherwise
   (`POST /api/moderation/cases/:id/actions`). A `moderation_actions` row and an
   `audit_log` row are written and the author is notified without naming the moderator.
4. As the author, open `/account/moderation` to see the decision and appeal; a different
   staff member decides the appeal.

`/admin?area=complaints` (Quinn or the admin) holds the seed's two complaints, each filed
by a candidate against a recruiter they actually dealt with, with the count of earlier
complaints against the same person and the ratings between the two. The playbook is
[moderation-playbook.md](moderation-playbook.md).

## 7. The admin console

Sign in as `admin@example.com`: every area is open, including settings (publish threshold,
recruiter quotas and cooldowns, message request limits, trust windows, moderation
thresholds, pre-moderation, the free-mail list) and the audit log, where every step above
left an entry with its actor.
